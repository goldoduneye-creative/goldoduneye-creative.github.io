About Me

A customer-focused professional with practical operations experience.

My background combines customer service, client communication, logistics support, service coordination and business operations.

Professional background

People-first service, backed by organised processes.

I have developed my customer service experience through roles that required consistent communication, follow-up, coordination and problem solving. I have supported customers through WhatsApp, email, telephone and social media while also keeping track of orders, deliveries, bookings and service requests.

My approach is simple: understand the customer's need, communicate clearly, take ownership of the next step and follow through until the issue or request has been properly addressed.

My additional background in event planning, catering and decoration has strengthened my ability to coordinate people, vendors, timelines and customer expectations in busy environments.

Professional strengths
  • Clear and respectful customer communication
  • Consistent customer follow-up
  • Complaint handling and escalation
  • Order, delivery and service tracking
  • Record keeping and administrative support
  • Managing multiple priorities and deadlines
  • Remote communication and independent work
How I work

Professional habits that support a dependable customer experience.

Listen & Understand

I take time to understand the customer's actual request before deciding the next action.

Communicate Clearly

I provide practical updates and keep customers informed rather than leaving requests unresolved.

Follow Through

I track open tasks and coordinate the required next steps until the customer has a clear outcome.
